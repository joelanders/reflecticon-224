# RMX16 hardware panel: polished against the photos

**Status:** done, 2026-11-20. The kit's RMX16 hardware panel (JUCE editor and web page; the same
`rmx16_layout()`) now matches the reference photos in look, not just in layout, without the maker's name or logo. Screenshot:
`2026-11-20-1818-rmx16-hardware-panel.png` (JUCE, booted v3.2). The photos themselves stay local
(`~/code/00-ams-dmx/photos/rmx16/`, not for publishing).

## What changed
- **No brand names (later the same day).** The maker's plate is left empty and the model strip
  reads only "model rmx 16 : digital reverberation system"; the DMX 15-80 panel likewise lost its
  "ams" plate and the maker's name and town. A first version drew the badge as vector art; it was
  removed at the user's request. The kit keeps the element it added for that, `PanelElement::Path`
  (SVG path data in panel mm, filled with `colour`; JUCE `Drawable::parseSVGPath`, web `<path d>`),
  for other printed artwork. The screenshots in these notes were re-rendered without the names.
- **Positions re-measured** (retrogear-2952 for the panel, the lit soundgas photo for the display):
  - The in knob and o/flo LEDs were about 4 mm off: in is now x 47.5 and the LEDs x 66.4, so the
    in / LEDs / out spacing is even, as on the panel.
  - The display window moved up (y 12.6 to 31.8) and narrowed to 144.6 to 347.2.
  - Digits are 0.3" parts (7.9 mm glyphs, previously 10.4 mm cells). The centres were measured from
    the lit segments and cross-checked against the dim segments of the unlit unit. Digits 2 to 4
    moved about 2 mm left and digits 8, 10 and 12 about 2 to 3 mm right.
  - The alphanumerics are 4.2 mm glyphs at x 308 to 342.
  - The pot knob is at (117.8, 22.2). Single-line key labels are at y 44.4.
  - The model strip spans 33.3 to 452.8, aligned with the sections above it.
- **Sizes.**
  - The knobs are 19.5 mm skirts (previously 14.5) with larger dial numerals.
  - The o/flo LEDs are 3.8 mm and the power LED 3.6 mm.
  - The power toggle is drawn larger; the toggle drawing now scales with the element.
- **Screws.** The section screws seen in the photos were added: in/out top-left, pot bottom-left,
  three display-section corners, the one between decay time and decay filter, and the power column.
- **Lettering.** Futura Bold for the printed legends (`PanelLayout::legend_weight = 700`); key caps
  stay Medium. The strip text is letter-spaced like the panel's.
- **Unlit LEDs** show their coloured lens a little brighter, in both renderers.

## Kit changes (affect every panel)
- `size` is now the true cap height in both renderers. JUCE previously set Futura's ascent+descent
  height (1.30 em), which drew text at about 81% of the stated size, while the web page drew about
  105%. Both now use em = cap / 0.76 (Futura's cap height). JUCE does this through
  `FontOptions::withPointHeight`.
  - All DMX 15-80 sizes were scaled by 0.81, so its JUCE panel looks the same as before; its web
    panel's text is now the same size as the JUCE panel's.
  - Default sizes: legends 2.1, dial numerals 2.2 (`Pot.size` sets them per knob).
  - Key-cap legends are 0.35 × key height.
- The web text keeps runs of spaces (`white-space: pre`), as JUCE does.
- `kw_panel_layout` exports `weight` and the new `path` kind.

## Checks
- `rmx16_check_machine` and `dx1580_check_machine`: all passed.
- pluginval strictness 10: SUCCESS for RMX16Kit and DX1580Kit.
- `web/build.sh` rebuilt all four modules; `node_check` passed for rmx16 and dmx1580.
- In the browser, the SVG panel's text widths match the JUCE editor's ("program" 19.4 vs 19 mm).
